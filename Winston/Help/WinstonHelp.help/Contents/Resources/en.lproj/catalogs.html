Catalogs & Series Finder

Catalog Hub searches OPDS 1 and OPDS 2 catalogs without mixing their books into your library. Winston includes Project Gutenberg, Standard Ebooks, Unglue.it, and a localized Wikisource, and you can add public or private OPDS sources.

Localized open sources

  • Unglue.it uses its public OPDS feed for browsing, title search, pagination, metadata, and downloads.
  • Wikisource follows Winston's effective language: Czech uses Czech Wikisource and English uses English Wikisource. Other future system languages fall back to English Wikisource.
  • Wikisource search calls the public MediaWiki Action API. Merely searching never asks WS Export to build or download a book.
  • Choosing Import for a Wikisource result explicitly requests an EPUB from WS Export with contributor credits enabled. Catalog Hub and Import Review show the source page, attribution, and contributor names; the saved file keeps the source-page provenance.

Add or manage a catalog

  1. Open Settings → Catalogs and click Add Catalog.
  2. Enter a name and the catalog's root URL. Choose Basic only when the provider explicitly gives you a username and password.
  3. Click Test Catalog. Testing can discover one advertised OPDS address from a normal website, but never changes your saved list.
  4. Save the catalog, then enable, disable, or reorder it. Only enabled catalogs participate in unified search.

Use Reset Built-ins to restore Winston's four supplied addresses and their order. Your custom catalogs remain untouched.

Authentication, HTTPS & privacy

  • Prefer HTTPS. Basic authentication is accepted only over HTTPS, and passwords are stored in the macOS Keychain.
  • An HTTP catalog requires explicit confirmation. Its traffic is not encrypted, and Winston will not send credentials to it.
  • Credentials are sent only to the exact HTTPS origin you configured (same scheme, host, and port). They are stripped on cross-origin redirects, and HTTPS-to-HTTP redirects are blocked.
  • Catalog queries and downloads go directly to the selected catalog. Imported books and local ownership matching stay on this Mac. Disable online metadata in Settings to stop catalog requests.

Unified search and Series Finder

  • Choose Catalogs in the sidebar and enter a title, author, or ISBN. Press ⌘F to focus search and Escape to cancel.
  • Results appear as each catalog finishes. A failed source does not hide successful results from the others.
  • Winston groups only conservative matches and keeps every source, edition, format, price, and acquisition relationship visible.
  • Use filters for catalog, language, format, acquisition, and local ownership. “Owned local edition” can also mean that another edition of the same work is in your library.
  • From a series, wishlist item, update notice, Discover card, or book menu, choose Find in Catalogs. Winston prefills the strongest available identifier, usually ISBN, then title and author.

Acquisition types and import

  • Open access and direct acquisition links can be imported when their advertised format is EPUB, MOBI, AZW, AZW3, PDF, TXT, HTML, or another format Winston explicitly supports.
  • Sample, borrow, buy, and subscribe links open at their source. Winston does not automate storefront, lending, or DRM workflows.
  • Choose the exact source and format you want. Unsupported or cross-origin authenticated downloads remain available as Open Source, not as unsafe import buttons.
  • Every catalog download opens Import Review. Metadata extracted from the file remains authoritative; catalog suggestions and differences are shown for review. Source attribution and contributor credits remain visible there. Canceling removes the temporary download and changes nothing in the library.
  • With a Kindle connected, Import and Send to Kindle waits for a successful library import before placing the new book in the normal transfer queue.

Troubleshooting

  • Authentication failed: edit the catalog and enter the current username and a new password. Saved passwords are never displayed.
  • Browse only: the catalog did not advertise compatible remote search. Open it and use its shelves and navigation instead.
  • No searchable catalogs: enable at least one catalog with search support, then retry.
  • Redirect blocked: open the source in a browser or ask the provider for its current HTTPS OPDS root. Winston never forwards a password to a different server.
  • Import unavailable: choose another advertised format or open the acquisition at its source. DRM and transactional links are not importable.
